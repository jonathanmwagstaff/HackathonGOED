Three.js – WebGL GPGPU Birds / Flocking Example

Original demo: threejs.org/examples/webgl_gpgpu_birds.html

Source repository: github.com/mrdoob/three.js

The hexagon boid animation on this site is derived from the Three.js GPGPU birds example. The core GPU-based flocking simulation (separation, alignment, cohesion), the GPUComputationRenderer utility, and the overall render architecture are copied or adapted from the original work with the following modifications:

  • Bird wing geometry replaced with flat hexagonal meshes rendered as camera-facing billboards.
  • Wing-flap vertex animation replaced with a speed-proportional leaf-flutter wobble.
  • Velocity shader extended with a homing force that guides each boid to an assigned target position along the outline of the State of Utah.
  • Lat / lon coordinates projected to scene space with a cosine-corrected flat-plane mapping (no globe curvature).
